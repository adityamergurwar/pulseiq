# Five-minute board demo script

This is a recording guide, not a recording. Record it only after a public URL has been configured.

| Time | Screen | Talk track |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:30 | Workforce overview | Introduce PulseIQ as the analytics cockpit for fictional Zenith Nova. State the data cutoff: 2026-09-30. |
| 0:30-1:10 | Workforce and attendance | Show 441 month-end employees and 95.2% September attendance. Point out that the dashboard titles state the finding and each figure comes from a governed metric. |
| 1:10-1:50 | Attrition and risk | Separate recorded exits from the model's grouped prediction. Explain that 43 people are in the High band, and that this is a prediction, not a fact or employee-level decision. |
| 1:50-2:30 | Payroll and budget | Show September payroll of INR 55.52m and D01's INR 214,404 overspend. Explain that positive variance means actual cost above budget and that the clean payroll-to-ledger comparison reconciles for 18 months. |
| 2:30-3:10 | P&L and cash | Show September net profit of INR 14.99m, open receivables of INR 139.39m, and DSO of 48 days. Note that cash movement is ledger movement because no opening bank balance was supplied. |
| 3:10-3:50 | Team productivity | Open SP-01 and compare T01 (162 completed points; 4.64-day cycle time) with T02 (210 points; 4.79-day cycle time). Say velocity should not be compared without considering work mix. |
| 3:50-4:25 | Data quality and lineage | Show row counts, quarantines, zero declared orphans and the DQ findings. Explain that invalid source rows are visible and counted, and the release gate requires review of the seeded raw issues. |
| 4:25-5:00 | Recommendation | Recommend reviewing D01 hiring/overtime against budget, supporting low-attendance teams, and prioritizing overdue receivables. End by stating that forecast accuracy is not available until actual months overlap the supplied forecast horizon. |

Do not say the app is live until the public URL works from another device. Do not display account passwords, secret values or individual predicted-risk names.
